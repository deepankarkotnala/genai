# Refund policy

Tags: billing, returns, refund, money

A refund may be issued when **all** of the following hold:

1. The order is within **30 days** of purchase.
2. The order status is `delivered` or `in_transit`.
3. The order has not already been refunded.
4. A human approver has authorised the amount.

Refunds are never issued automatically. An agent may **recommend** a refund and
prepare the request, but the transfer itself requires explicit approval. If any
condition fails, explain which one and offer the alternatives below.

Alternatives when a refund is not permitted: account credit, a discount on the
next cycle, or escalation to a billing specialist.

Duplicate charges are the one case where the 30-day window is measured from the
**duplicate** charge, not the original purchase.
