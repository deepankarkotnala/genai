Top 50 DSA Interview Questions

If you only have time for one list, use this one. Fifty problems, drawn from the 150 high-frequency set, chosen so that every interview pattern is covered at least once and no pattern is covered twice for no reason.

How this 50 was chosen

Pattern coverage first, difficulty last

Three rules. Every pattern appears — all sixteen groups below, so no interview question can hit a technique you have never practised. Easy problems stay in where they are the clearest way into a pattern: Contains Duplicate teaches the seen-set, Climbing Stairs teaches the DP recurrence, and skipping them to look serious just makes the medium ones harder. Nothing rated Hard — problems like Trapping Rain Water and Median of Two Sorted Arrays are famous rather than representative, and they cost hours that pattern coverage would spend better. They are still in the 150 if you want them.

How to work through it

Go pattern by pattern, not top to bottom. Do the easy problem in a group first, name the pattern out loud, then do the mediums — they will feel like the same problem wearing a different hat. Every title links to the chapter that teaches it, with the full worked solution and complexity reasoning. Finished these? Continue with the 150 high-frequency set.
