High-Frequency DSA Questions

The full high-frequency set — the pattern-defining problems most asked at FAANG/MAANG and major India product companies. It is known as the 150 list. 140 problems have a full write-up here; three more are listed below and marked not yet in the curriculum. This is the list the ★ markers throughout the curriculum refer to. Short on time, or starting out? Do the Top 50 first; it is drawn from this list and marked ★ below.

Who this list is for

Use the 150 when you have weeks rather than days, when you are targeting product companies with hard coding rounds, or when you have finished the Top 50 and want depth in a pattern you know is weak. It includes the Hard problems the Top 50 deliberately leaves out.
