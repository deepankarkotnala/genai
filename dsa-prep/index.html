A better practice loop

1

Recognise the signal

Before coding, name the pattern out loud and the invariant you will maintain. Speed comes from recognition, not memorisation.

2

State the trade-off

Give brute force, then the optimisation and why it is safe — including auxiliary memory, output space and mutation.

3

Test before you claim done

Empty, single element, duplicates, negatives, already-sorted. Use practice mode to hide solutions and rebuild from the pattern.
