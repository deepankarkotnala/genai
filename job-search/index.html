Where to Find Remote Roles

Preparation is only half the problem — you still have to find companies willing to hire someone in India for full-time remote work. Every board below carries IT and engineering roles, with AI, ML and GenAI positions specifically. Freelance marketplaces are deliberately left out.

⚠️
Read this before you apply anywhere

Most “remote” roles posted by US and EU companies are remote within their own country or region only — the listing often says Remote (US) and it means it. Filter for “worldwide” or “hiring in India” before spending time on an application. Where a company does hire globally, they almost always pay you through an Employer of Record such as Deel, Remote.com or Oyster, or engage you as an independent contractor. Find out which one is on offer, because it changes your taxes, your notice period and whether you get any benefits at all.

India-headquartered. Places Indian professionals into long-term full-time remote roles with overseas companies and handles the contracting.

Matches India-based engineers to US companies and AI labs. Pass the vetting once, then get matched. Engagements are usually long-term contracts rather than employment — check before signing.

How to tell a real listing from a scam

1

Nobody legitimate asks you to pay

No real employer charges a registration, training, equipment or security-deposit fee. A request for money at any stage ends the conversation.

2

Verify the company independently

Find the role on the company's own careers page. Check the recruiter has a real LinkedIn history. Interviews held only over chat, with an offer inside a day, are a scam pattern.

3

Get the payment structure in writing

Ask early: employee via an EOR, or independent contractor? In which currency, and who absorbs the conversion cost? Vague answers here reliably predict payment problems later.

A note on India-domestic boards

Naukri, Instahyre, Cutshort and LinkedIn India are the right tools for a role in India, and they are far better at that than anything above. This page is specifically about working from India for a company based elsewhere — a different search with different mechanics. Do not run one search expecting it to serve both.
