How a question earns its place

A question is kept if recent 2025–2026 interview reports or current question lists show it being asked, if it is a fundamental every Python round touches, or if it maps to a skill named in current India job descriptions. Anything that only appears in old material, or repeats a question already on the page, is cut.

How to use this bank

1

Answer before you open it

Read the question, say your answer out loud, then open the panel. Reading answers you never attempted feels productive and teaches almost nothing.

2

Learn the 30-second version

Interviews reward a clear short answer followed by a pause. The long answer is for understanding; the 30-second one is what you actually say.

3

Prepare the follow-up

Every question lists the follow-up that usually comes next. Being ready for it is what separates a good answer from a strong one.
