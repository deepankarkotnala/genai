# Proration

Tags: billing, proration, invoice, upgrade, downgrade

When a plan changes mid-cycle the invoice covers two parts: the unused portion
of the old plan is credited, and the remainder of the cycle on the new plan is
charged. The invoice total is therefore rarely the headline plan price.

## Invoice higher than the quote

An invoice that is higher than the quote is usually the upgrade charge plus tax
on the remaining days. Walk the customer through the two line items before
treating it as a billing error.

## Downgrades

Downgrades issue credit against the next invoice rather than a refund.
