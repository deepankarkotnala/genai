# Shipping delays

Tags: shipping, delivery, tracking, delay, parcel

Tracking that has not updated for more than three days counts as a stalled
shipment, even when the carrier still shows the parcel as in transit.

## Handling order


1. Confirm the order status is `in_transit`.
2. Offer a replacement at no cost if the value is under 100.00.
3. Above that value, escalate to the logistics queue rather than promising a
   replacement.

## What not to promise

Do not promise a delivery date the carrier has not confirmed. "I have asked the
carrier to investigate and will update you within one working day" is accurate;
"it will arrive tomorrow" usually is not.
