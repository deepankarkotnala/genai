How to learn here

Four steps for every topic. The last two are what turn reading into remembering.

📖

1 · Read Level 1

Every topic opens with a short core idea you can finish in about 30 minutes, with a worked example. Levels 2 and 3 go deeper when you are ready.

🧠

2 · Check yourself

Each level ends with recall questions. Answer out loud before you open the answer - recalling is what makes it stick; re-reading is not.

🔁

3 · Review on days 1, 3, 7 and 21

Say the topic's memory hook, then run its review cards. A few minutes at growing gaps keeps a topic for months.

🎤

4 · Practise

Use the question banks, drills and design scenarios: the same ideas, asked the way colleagues and interviewers ask them.
